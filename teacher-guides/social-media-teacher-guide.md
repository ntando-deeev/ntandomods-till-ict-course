## TEACHING SOCIAL MEDIA TO SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS

Students already USE social media personally. Your job is to shift their mindset from personal user to business tool user.

**Opening Activity (Day 1):**
Ask: "Who has a Facebook account?" (most hands go up)
Ask: "Who has used Facebook to SELL something?" (fewer hands)
Ask: "Who has made money from social media?" (even fewer)
> "By the end of this course, all three hands should be yours."

---

## KEY TEACHING APPROACHES

### Show Real Zimbabwe Business Examples
- Find 5 successful Zimbabwean businesses on Facebook before the course
- Show their pages: what they post, how they engage, their follower count
- Ask students: "What are they doing right? What could be better?"

### Canva Teaching (Critical)
- Create a Canva account on the training computer/phone in advance
- First lesson: just explore. No pressure to create anything perfect.
- Second lesson: follow a template. Customise colours and text only.
- Third lesson: design from scratch.

### The "Before and After" Activity
Students take a photo of a product on a plain white background vs a well-staged photo.
Then they design a Canva post with each photo.
The difference in quality is instantly visible — teaches presentation without words.

## WEEK-BY-WEEK NOTES

### Week 1 — Platform Setup
- Every student MUST leave Week 1 with a Facebook Business Page and Instagram Business account
- Don't move to content until setup is complete
- Profile photo and cover photo must be set before leaving Day 1

### Week 2 — Content
- Students create actual content for their real or fictional business
- Run a class "content critique" — students show their posts, class gives feedback
- Teach the 3-2-1 content rule visually with a calendar

### Week 3 — Growth & Ads
- If budget allows, run ONE $2 Facebook boost as a class demonstration
- Show live insights from a page — the numbers make it real
- End with the "negative review" role play — this always generates great discussion
