## 🎯 Month Overview

The final month prepares learners to enter the job market or start their own business. From CV writing to job interviews to business planning, this month is about turning skills into income — the NtandoMods way.

---

## 📚 Weekly Breakdown

### Week 21 — Professional CV & Job Applications
- What employers in Zimbabwe actually look for
- Building a professional CV: structure, length, language
  - Personal details
  - Summary statement
  - Skills section
  - Work experience
  - Education and training
  - References
- Cover letters: how to write one for retail and office jobs
- Where to find jobs in Zimbabwe:
  - Indeed Zimbabwe
  - LinkedIn
  - The Herald classifieds
  - Facebook job groups
  - Walk-in applications
- Job application emails: how to send your CV professionally
- Following up after applying
- **Practical:** Write your own professional CV in Word

### Week 22 — Job Interview Preparation
- Types of interviews: face-to-face, panel, phone, WhatsApp video
- Common interview questions for retail and office jobs:
  - "Tell me about yourself"
  - "Why do you want this job?"
  - "What are your strengths and weaknesses?"
  - "Describe a time you handled a difficult customer"
  - "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?"
- How to answer using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Professional dress code for interviews in Zimbabwe
- Body language: eye contact, posture, handshake, smile
- Punctuality and preparation
- Questions to ask the interviewer
- Salary negotiation basics
- **Practical:** Mock interview sessions (in pairs, recorded for self-review)

### Week 23 — Starting Your Own Business in Zimbabwe
- Identifying business opportunities in Zimbabwe
- Top low-capital business ideas for NtandoMods graduates:
  - Tuckshop/spaza shop
  - Mobile airtime and data reselling
  - Printing and photocopying services
  - Social media management for small businesses
  - Bookkeeping services for small traders
  - WhatsApp delivery service
- Writing a simple business plan:
  - Business name and idea
  - Target market
  - Startup costs
  - Expected revenue
  - Marketing strategy
- Registering your business in Zimbabwe (PRAZ, ZIMRA, local council)
- Opening a business bank account (Steward Bank, FBC, CBZ)
- Microfinance and startup funding options in Zimbabwe (SMEDCO, Women's Bank, CBZ Agro)
- **Practical:** Write a 2-page business plan for a business idea of your choice

### Week 24 — Final Diploma Examination & Graduation

- NtandoMods Diploma Certificate Presentation

## 🎯 STAR Method for Interview Answers

| Letter | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **S** | Situation | "I was working the till during month-end rush..." |
| **T** | Task | "A customer was angry about a pricing error..." |
| **A** | Action | "I stayed calm, called my supervisor, and offered a price check..." |
| **R** | Result | "The customer was satisfied and thanked me before leaving." |
