# 📅 Month 3 — Microsoft Office Suite
**Programme:** 6-Month Diploma | **Duration:** 4 Weeks  
**Powered by NtandoMods**

---

## 🎯 Month Overview

Month 3 is full immersion in Microsoft Office — the most in-demand software skill for office and retail jobs in Zimbabwe. By the end, learners can produce professional Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint presentations, and manage email with Outlook.

---

## 📚 Weekly Breakdown

### Week 9 — Microsoft Word (Advanced)
- Page layout: margins, orientation, page size (A4 standard)
- Headers and footers with page numbers
- Tables of contents (automatic)
- Mail merge — sending personalised letters to multiple people
- Track Changes and Comments (for reviewing documents)
- Templates: creating reusable business templates
- **Practical:** Create a professional letter template for a retail business
- **Practical:** Use mail merge to send 20 "Customer Reward" letters

### Week 10 — Microsoft Excel (Advanced)
- IF statements: =IF(condition, value_if_true, value_if_false)
- VLOOKUP: finding data across sheets
- Conditional formatting: colour cells based on values
- Charts and graphs: bar, pie, line charts from sales data
- Multiple worksheets: linking data between sheets
- Protecting cells and sheets (password protection)
- Printing: print areas, headers, gridlines, fit to page
- **Practical:** Build a complete monthly sales dashboard
- **Practical:** Create a staff wages calculator using IF formulas

### Week 11 — Microsoft PowerPoint
- Creating a professional presentation from scratch
- Slide layouts and design themes
- Adding text, images, charts, and tables to slides
- Slide transitions and animations (professional, not distracting)
- Speaker notes
- Presenting in slideshow mode
- Exporting as PDF for sharing
- **Practical:** Create a 10-slide business presentation on "Why NtandoMods Skills Programme?"

### Week 12 — Microsoft Outlook & Professional Communication
- Setting up Outlook with a Gmail or business email
- Organising your inbox: folders, rules, flags
- Calendar: scheduling meetings and appointments
- Contacts: maintaining a professional contact list
- Writing professional emails: tone, format, etiquette
- Email signatures
- Handling email attachments safely
- CC vs BCC — when to use each
- **Month 3 Test & Portfolio Task**

---

## 📝 Month 3 Assessment

**Written Test:** 1 hour | 50 marks | Pass: 60%  
**Portfolio Task:** Submit a complete "Business Pack" containing:
1. A professional letter (Word)
2. A monthly sales report with chart (Excel)
3. A 10-slide company presentation (PowerPoint)
4. Screenshot of organised Outlook inbox
