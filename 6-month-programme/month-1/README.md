**Programme:** 6-Month Diploma | **Duration:** 4 Weeks  
**Powered by NtandoMods**

## 🎯 Month Overview

Month 1 builds a solid foundation in retail and takes till operating to a professional level. Learners understand the full retail environment, advanced POS features, and Zimbabwe-specific retail regulations.

---

## 📚 Weekly Breakdown

### Week 1 — The Retail Industry in Zimbabwe
- The retail sector in Zimbabwe (supermarkets, pharmacies, fuel, clothing)
- Major retailers: OK Zimbabwe, Pick n Pay, Choppies, TM, Edgars, Jet
- The economy of retail — how shops make profit
- VAT (Value Added Tax) in Zimbabwe — how it works at the till
- ZIMRA requirements for retail businesses
- Retail employment law — your rights as a worker

### Week 2 — Advanced POS & Till Management
- Deep dive into POS software (GAAP, AccPac, Sievert, generic systems)
- Managing multiple payment methods simultaneously
- Price overrides and manager functions
- Processing layaways and deposits
- Bulk transaction handling during peak hours
- Shift management: opening, mid-shift audit, closing

### Week 3 — Advanced Cash Handling & Loss Prevention
- Advanced counterfeit detection techniques
- Using UV lights and note-checking machines
- Reconciling EcoCash merchant statements
- Understanding shrinkage and stock loss
- CCTV awareness and how surveillance protects you
- Completing incident reports

### Week 4 — Customer Relations & Retail Law
- The Consumer Protection Act (Zimbabwe) — what customers are entitled to
- Handling formal complaints
- The store returns and exchanges policy framework
- Loyalty programmes: how they work and how to process them
- Disability and accessibility at the till
- **Month 1 Test & Portfolio Task**

## 📖 Key Concepts This Month

- **VAT** = 15% tax added to most goods in Zimbabwe
- **Shrinkage** = loss of stock due to theft, damage, or errors
- **Layaway** = customer pays deposit to reserve an item
- **Consumer Protection Act** = law protecting buyers in Zimbabwe
- **Z-Read** = end-of-day POS report
