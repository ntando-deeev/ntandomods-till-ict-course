# 📅 Month 4 — Internet, Digital Business & Social Media
**Programme:** 6-Month Diploma | **Duration:** 4 Weeks  
**Powered by NtandoMods**

---

## 🎯 Month Overview

Month 4 transforms learners into digitally capable business people. From running a WhatsApp Business shop to Facebook advertising and Google tools, learners understand how digital tools grow real businesses in Zimbabwe.

---

## 📚 Weekly Breakdown

### Week 13 — Digital Business Tools (Zimbabwe-Focused)
- Google Workspace: Docs, Sheets, Forms, Drive
- Cloud storage: what it is and why it matters
- Google Forms: creating customer feedback forms, order forms
- Google Sheets vs Excel: when to use which
- Canva (free): creating social media posts, flyers, price lists
- QR codes: creating QR codes for your business (menu, payment, contact)
- Online banking in Zimbabwe: CBZ, Steward Bank, FBC app navigation

### Week 14 — WhatsApp Business Mastery
- Full WhatsApp Business setup and optimisation
- Catalogue management: uploading products with photos and prices
- Broadcast lists vs Groups: when to use each for marketing
- Status updates as a marketing tool
- Labels and conversation management
- WhatsApp Pay for Business (where available)
- Handling customer complaints professionally on WhatsApp
- Response time standards and auto-reply setup
- **Practical:** Set up a complete WhatsApp Business shop for a fictional grocery store

### Week 15 — Facebook & Instagram for Business
- Creating a Facebook Business Page (not personal profile)
- Page optimisation: about section, profile photo, cover photo, CTA button
- Types of posts: photos, videos, reels, stories, text updates
- Understanding Facebook's algorithm — what gets seen
- Facebook Marketplace: listing products for sale in Zimbabwe
- Instagram Business account setup
- Hashtag strategy for Zimbabwe audiences
- Responding to comments and DMs professionally
- When NOT to post (common mistakes Zimbabwean businesses make)
- **Practical:** Create a Facebook Business Page and make 3 professional posts

### Week 16 — Online Safety, Data & Privacy
- GDPR and Zimbabwe data protection basics
- What customer data you can and cannot collect
- Protecting your business accounts: 2FA on all platforms
- What to do if your business account is hacked
- Email marketing basics: building a contact list ethically
- Understanding analytics: Facebook Insights, WhatsApp stats
- **Month 4 Test & Portfolio Task**

---

## 📝 Month 4 Assessment

**Written Test:** 1 hour | 50 marks | Pass: 60%  
**Portfolio Task:** Submit a "Digital Business Starter Pack" for a fictional Zimbabwean business:
1. WhatsApp Business profile screenshot with 5+ catalogue items
2. Facebook Business Page with 3 professional posts
3. A Canva-designed A5 flyer for the business
4. A Google Form customer satisfaction survey (5 questions)
