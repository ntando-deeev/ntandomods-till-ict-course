# 📅 Month 5 — Business Skills & Accounting Basics
**Programme:** 6-Month Diploma | **Duration:** 4 Weeks  
**Powered by NtandoMods**

---

## 🎯 Month Overview

Month 5 elevates learners from till operators and computer users to business-minded professionals. They learn bookkeeping, invoicing, stock control, and how money flows through a small retail business — making them extremely valuable to any employer.

---

## 📚 Weekly Breakdown

### Week 17 — Business Basics & Money Management
- What is a business? Types of businesses in Zimbabwe
- Sole trader, partnership, private company (Pvt Ltd) — differences
- Business registration in Zimbabwe (ZIMRA, Companies & Other Business Entities Act)
- Revenue, expenses, profit, and loss — explained simply
- Cash flow: why profitable businesses can still fail
- Break-even point: how many sales do I need to cover costs?
- **Example:** A tuckshop owner in Mbare — tracking income and expenses
- **Practical:** Calculate profit/loss for a fictional tuckshop using Excel

### Week 18 — Basic Bookkeeping & Invoicing
- Double-entry bookkeeping concept (simplified)
- The Cash Book: recording every income and expense
- Categories of expenses: rent, wages, electricity, stock, transport
- Creating professional invoices in Word and Excel
- Invoice components: invoice number, date, itemised list, totals, payment terms
- Receipts vs Invoices — what is the difference
- Recording and tracking invoices in Excel
- **Practical:** Create an invoice template in Word and record 10 invoices in Excel
- **Practical:** Create a simple cash book for a month of business transactions

### Week 19 — Stock Management & Ordering
- What is stock/inventory management?
- The stock count process: how, when, and why
- Stock control sheet in Excel: opening stock, received, sold, closing stock
- Minimum stock levels and reorder points
- Dealing with suppliers in Zimbabwe: comparing prices, credit terms
- Stock loss causes: theft, damage, expiry, miscounting
- FIFO method: First In, First Out — why older stock sells first
- Purchase orders: creating and sending a PO to a supplier
- **Practical:** Conduct a mock stock count and record results in Excel

### Week 20 — Payroll & Staff Records
- Basic payroll concepts: gross pay, deductions, net pay
- NSSA (National Social Security Authority) contributions
- PAYE (Pay As You Earn) — income tax deductions
- Creating a simple payroll spreadsheet in Excel
- Staff records: what information to keep, how to keep it organised
- Leave management: annual leave, sick leave tracking
- **Month 5 Test & Portfolio Task**

## 📊 Key Financial Terms

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|-----------------|
| **Revenue** | Money coming IN from sales |
| **Expenses** | Money going OUT (costs) |
| **Profit** | Revenue minus expenses (what you keep) |
| **Cash flow** | Money moving in and out over time |
| **Invoice** | A bill you send to a customer |
| **PAYE** | Income tax paid by employees |
| **NSSA** | Social security contribution (employer + employee) |
| **Stock** | Products you sell |
| **FIFO** | Sell oldest stock first |
