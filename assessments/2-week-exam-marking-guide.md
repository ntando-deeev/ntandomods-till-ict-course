### Question 3 — Short Answer (15 marks)

**3.1 — 10 Steps of a Sale (5 marks — 0.5 per correct step)**
1. Greet the customer warmly
2. Scan all items one by one
3. Check screen — make sure all items are correct
4. Announce the total to the customer
5. Ask how they would like to pay
6. Receive payment
7. Process payment on the system
8. Give change (if cash)
9. Print and hand over receipt
10. Thank the customer and say goodbye

**3.2 — Void vs Refund (4 marks)**
- Void: cancelled BEFORE payment is received. Example: cashier scans wrong item, customer hasn't paid yet — press VOID. *(2 marks)*
- Refund: processed AFTER payment. Example: customer bought item and paid, comes back to return it — process a refund. *(2 marks)*

**3.3 — Handling the cereal return (3 marks)**
- Ask to see the receipt — verify purchase *(1 mark)*
- Note that the product is half used — this affects the return decision *(1 mark)*
- Call supervisor — half-used item may not qualify for full refund, supervisor must decide *(1 mark)*
- *Award 1 mark for any other reasonable professional step*

**3.4 — Signs of counterfeit note (3 marks, 1 each)**
Any 3 of:
- Feels too smooth/like plain paper
- No watermark visible when held to light
- No security thread
- Colours look faded or off
- No texture when rubbing the print
- Blurry or uneven serial numbers
- No colour-shifting ink on denomination

### Question 5 — Long Answer (10 marks)

**5.1a — Type of fraud (1 mark)**
- Short-change fraud / Change-raising scam *(1 mark)*

**5.1b — Handling the situation (4 marks)**
Award marks for any of: stop and recount all money carefully before proceeding; stay calm and don't let rush pressure force a mistake; lay the original change on the counter first before accepting more; involve supervisor if confused; refuse to give extra change if you are not certain. *(1 mark each, up to 4)*

**5.1c — Prevention (2 marks)**
- Always count change carefully and announce it aloud before closing the drawer *(1 mark)*
- Never accept additional notes/coins once change has been given without calling a supervisor *(1 mark)*

**5.1d — Aggressive customer (3 marks)**
- Stay calm and do not argue *(1 mark)*
- Call supervisor or security immediately *(1 mark)*
- Do not put yourself at risk — step back from the till if necessary *(1 mark)*
