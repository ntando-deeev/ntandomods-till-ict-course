### Section 1 — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

1. "Revenue" means: A) Profit after expenses B) Total money coming in from sales C) Business bank balance D) Stock value

2. FIFO stands for: A) Finance In, Finance Out B) First In, First Out C) Fixed Income Financial Operations D) Full Inventory For Order

3. NSSA is: A) National Stock & Sales Authority B) National Social Security Authority C) National Sales Support Agency D) No Such State Agency

4. A "cash flow" problem means: A) No profit being made B) Not enough money available right now, even if the business is profitable C) Too much stock D) No customers

5. A "purchase order" is: A) A bill sent to a customer B) An internal order form sent to a supplier to request goods C) A receipt from a bank D) A payment confirmation

6. PAYE stands for: A) Payments After Year End B) Pay As You Earn — income tax deducted from wages C) Pre-Approved Year Expenses D) Payroll Administration Year End

7. What does "break-even point" mean? A) When the business starts making profit B) When total revenue equals total costs (no profit, no loss) C) When the business closes D) When stock runs out

8. An "invoice" is: A) A receipt given after payment B) A bill sent to a customer requesting payment C) A bank statement D) A stock count sheet

9. Double-entry bookkeeping means: A) Every transaction is recorded twice — once as debit, once as credit B) Two people check each transaction C) You write in two books D) All entries are duplicated as backup

10. "Gross pay" is: A) Pay after deductions B) Pay before deductions C) Pay for overtime only D) Annual pay
