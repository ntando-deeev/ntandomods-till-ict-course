## 📖 Lesson 1.1 — What is Retail? (45 minutes)

**Retail** is the process of selling goods or services directly to customers for personal use.

### Types of Retail Businesses in Zimbabwe
| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| Supermarkets | OK Zimbabwe, Pick n Pay, Choppies, TM Pick n Pay |
| Pharmacies | Clicks, Econet Healthtown |
| Filling Stations | ZUBIA, Total, Shell, Puma |
| Hardware Shops | Bain's Hardware, Builders Warehouse |
| Clothing Stores | Jet, Edgars, Truworths |
| Small Businesses | Tuckshops, spaza shops, market stalls |

### The Retail Supply Chain
```
Manufacturer → Wholesaler → Retailer → Customer
```
- **Manufacturer** — makes the product (e.g. Cairns Foods making Mazoe)
- **Wholesaler** — buys in bulk and sells to shops (e.g. Metro Wholesale)
- **Retailer** — sells to the final customer (e.g. your local supermarket)
- **Customer** — the person who buys the product for use

## 📖 Lesson 1.2 — The Role of a Cashier (45 minutes)

A **cashier** is a person responsible for receiving payments from customers in exchange for goods or services.

### Core Responsibilities
1. Greeting and serving customers politely
2. Scanning or entering product prices correctly
3. Processing cash, card, and mobile money payments
4. Giving correct change
5. Issuing receipts
6. Balancing the till at end of shift
7. Reporting discrepancies to supervisors
8. Keeping the till area clean and organised

### What a Cashier Does NOT Do
- Set product prices (that is the manager's job)
- Handle stock on shelves (that is the stock controller's job)
- Approve refunds above a certain limit (supervisor approval needed)

## 📖 Lesson 1.3 — Qualities of a Good Cashier (45 minutes)

| Quality | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------------|
| Honesty | You handle real money — trust is everything |
| Accuracy | Wrong change = loss for the business or customer |
| Speed | Long queues make customers angry |
| Politeness | Good service brings customers back |
| Attention to detail | Spot fake notes, scan errors, overcharges |
| Calmness under pressure | Busy days, complaints, long queues |
| Punctuality | The till cannot open without you |

## 📖 Lesson 1.4 — Workplace Rules & Professional Conduct (45 minutes)

### Dress Code
- Always wear your uniform clean and ironed
- Name badge must be visible at all times
- No excessive jewellery that could interfere with work
- Hair must be neat and tied back if long

### Conduct at the Till
- No eating or drinking at the till
- No personal phone use during working hours
- Do not leave the till unattended without informing a supervisor
- Never open the till drawer without a transaction
- Be aware of your surroundings — theft can happen fast

### Workplace Ethics
- Report any suspicious activity to your supervisor immediately
- Never share your till password with anyone
- If you make an error, report it — do not hide it
- Treat all customers equally, regardless of how they look

## 🔄 Class Activity

**Group Discussion (30 mins):**  
Think of a shop you have visited in Zimbabwe. Describe:
1. What the cashier did well
2. What the cashier did poorly
3. How you would have done it better

**Role Play (30 mins):**  
In pairs — one person is the cashier, one is the customer. Practice:
- Greeting the customer
- Announcing the total
- Taking payment and giving change
- Saying goodbye professionally
