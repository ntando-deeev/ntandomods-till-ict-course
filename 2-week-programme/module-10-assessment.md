## 📖 Revision Summary — Week 1 (Till Operating)

### Key Concepts to Remember
- 10 Steps of processing a sale
- Types of payment in Zimbabwe: Cash, EcoCash, OneMoney, Zipit, Card
- Float = opening cash; Cash Up = end-of-shift count
- Void = cancel before payment; Refund = after payment
- GREAT customer service framework
- Counterfeit note detection methods
- Types of retail theft and how to prevent them

---

## 📖 Revision Summary — Week 2 (ICT Basics)

### Key Concepts to Remember
- ICT = Information and Communication Technology
- Input devices vs output devices
- Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+Z
- Word: bold, italic, underline, tables, saving, printing
- Excel: cells, formulas (=SUM, =AVERAGE, =MIN, =MAX)
- Gmail: professional email creation and writing
- WhatsApp Business: profile setup and catalogue
