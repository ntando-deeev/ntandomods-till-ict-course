# 📅 2-Week Short Course — Till Operating & ICT Basics
### Powered by NtandoMods | No Qualifications Required

---

## 🎯 Course Overview

This intensive 2-week programme equips learners with the practical skills needed to operate a cash register/till and use basic ICT tools in a retail or office environment. Perfect for immediate employment in supermarkets, shops, filling stations, and small businesses across Zimbabwe.

**Duration:** 10 working days (2 weeks)  
**Hours per day:** 4 hours  
**Total hours:** 40 hours  
**Fee:** $35 USD  
**Certificate:** NtandoMods Short Course Certificate
