## 📖 Lesson 9.1 — Using the Internet (45 minutes)

### What is the Internet?
The internet is a global network that connects computers, phones and devices so they can share information.

### Common Uses in Zimbabwean Business
- Finding supplier prices
- Checking exchange rates (USD/ZiG)
- Sending and receiving emails
- Online banking (CBZ, FBC, Steward Bank apps)
- Advertising on Facebook and WhatsApp
- Looking up regulations (ZIMRA, ZIMDEF)

### Using Google Chrome
1. Open Chrome (click the icon on desktop)
2. Click the address bar at the top
3. Type your search (e.g. "USD to ZiG rate today") or a website address
4. Press Enter
5. Read the results and click the most relevant link

### Safe Internet Use
- Only visit trusted websites
- Never enter your bank password on an unfamiliar website
- Lock your screen if you leave the computer
- Do not download files from unknown sources
- Be aware of online scams (fake EcoCash messages, phishing emails)

## 📖 Lesson 9.2 — Creating a Professional Email (45 minutes)

### Why You Need a Professional Email
- Employers expect it
- Needed for job applications
- Needed for business communication
- Free with Gmail (Google)

### Creating a Gmail Account
1. Go to **gmail.com**
2. Click "Create account"
3. Enter your name
4. Choose an email address — **use your real name!**
   - ✅ Good: `tendai.moyo@gmail.com`
   - ❌ Bad: `coolboy2009@gmail.com`
5. Set a strong password (mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
6. Complete verification with your phone number

### Keeping Your Account Secure
- Never share your password
- Always log out on shared computers
- Set up recovery options (backup email or phone number)

## 📖 Lesson 9.3 — Writing a Professional Email (60 minutes)

### Parts of a Professional Email
```
TO:      recipient@example.com
CC:      (other people who should see this)
SUBJECT: Clear, specific subject line

GREETING:   Dear Mr/Ms [Surname],

BODY:       First paragraph — state your purpose
            Second paragraph — provide details
            Third paragraph — call to action / next steps

CLOSING:    Kind regards,
            [Your Full Name]
            [Your Phone Number]
```

### Subject Line Examples
- ✅ "Job Application — Cashier Position — Tendai Moyo"
- ✅ "Invoice #00123 — June 2026 — NtandoMods"
- ❌ "hi" / "please read" / "urgent!!!!"

### Email Writing Practice
**Write a professional email for the following scenario:**
> You are applying for a cashier position at Choppies Supermarket in Bulawayo. The manager's name is Mr Dube and his email is recruitment@choppies.co.zw. You completed the NtandoMods Till Operating course and have this certificate.

## 📖 Lesson 9.4 — WhatsApp Business for Small Shops (45 minutes)

WhatsApp Business is a free app used by millions of Zimbabwean traders to run their businesses.

### Advantages Over Regular WhatsApp
- Business profile with shop name, address, hours, website
- Product catalogue — display items with prices and photos
- Quick Replies — save common messages
- Away Message — automatic reply when you're busy
- Labels — organise chats (New Order, Paid, Delivered, etc.)

### Setting Up WhatsApp Business
1. Download "WhatsApp Business" from Google Play Store
2. Register with your business phone number
3. Set up your business profile:
   - Business name
   - Category (Retail, Food, Services, etc.)
   - Address
   - Business hours
   - Profile photo (logo or shop photo)
4. Add your first 3 products to the catalogue

### Writing a Professional WhatsApp Message to a Customer
> "Good morning! Thank you for your order. Your total is $12.50. Please send EcoCash to 0772XXXXXX (Tendai Moyo). We will deliver within 2 hours. Thank you for choosing [Shop Name]! 🙏"
