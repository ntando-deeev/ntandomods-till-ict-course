## 📖 Lesson 4.1 — Counting Cash Professionally (60 minutes)

### The Professional Way to Count Notes
1. Fan the notes — check all face the same direction
2. Count by denomination (all $20s together, all $10s, etc.)
3. Count each denomination twice
4. Use a note-counting rubber on your fingers
5. Lay counted stacks flat, face down
6. Announce total to supervisor before banking

### Counting Coins
- Sort by denomination first
- Stack coins in groups of 10
- Count stacks then multiply

### Cash Counting Practice Drill
> Learners are given envelopes with mixed notes/coins and must count and record the total in under 3 minutes.

## 📖 Lesson 4.2 — The Till Float (45 minutes)

The **float** is the opening cash placed in the till before trading begins. It ensures you can give change to the first customers of the day.

### Typical Zimbabwe Retail Float
| Denomination | Quantity | Total |
|---|---|---|
| $20 notes | 2 | $40.00 |
| $10 notes | 2 | $20.00 |
| $5 notes | 4 | $20.00 |
| $2 notes | 5 | $10.00 |
| $1 notes | 10 | $10.00 |
| Coins (various) | assorted | $5.00 |
| **FLOAT TOTAL** | | **$105.00** |

### Float Rules
- Count your float at the START of your shift — sign the float sheet
- Never mix your float with personal money
- If float is short, report immediately — do not start trading
- Float must be returned at end of shift intact

## 📖 Lesson 4.3 — Identifying Fake Notes (45 minutes)

Zimbabwe uses both USD and ZiG. Counterfeit USD is a common problem.

### How to Check a USD Note (Security Features)
| Feature | What to Look For |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Watermark** | Hold to light — you should see the face portrait |
| **Security thread** | Thin metallic strip embedded in the note |
| **Colour-shifting ink** | The "100" or denomination numeral changes colour when tilted |
| **Raised printing** | You can feel the texture of the print |
| **Microprinting** | Tiny text visible under magnification |
| **UV light** | Under UV, genuine notes glow in specific places |

### Warning Signs of a Fake Note
- Feels too smooth (like plain paper)
- Colours look faded or off
- No texture when you run your finger across it
- Watermark is missing or printed on top of paper
- Serial number looks uneven or blurry

### What to Do With a Suspected Fake Note
1. Do NOT return the note to the customer
2. Do NOT accuse the customer loudly (it could be genuine)
3. Calmly call your supervisor
4. Let the supervisor handle it
5. Write an incident report

## 📖 Lesson 4.4 — End-of-Shift Cash Up (45 minutes)

**Cashing up** means counting all the money in your till at the end of your shift and comparing it to what the POS system says you should have.

### Cash Up Process
```
STEP 1: Print the Z-Read report from POS (end of day sales total)
STEP 2: Remove float from the till (set aside)
STEP 3: Count all remaining cash by denomination
STEP 4: Record totals on the Cash Up Sheet
STEP 5: Compare your count to the POS report
STEP 6: Report any differences (over or short) to supervisor
STEP 7: Bag the cash for banking and seal the bag
STEP 8: Sign the cash up sheet and hand to supervisor
```

### Understanding Discrepancies
| Situation | Meaning | Action |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Till is **over** | More cash than expected | Report — do not pocket the extra |
| Till is **short** | Less cash than expected | Report — investigate errors |
| Till **balances** | Perfect match | Great work! |

> A consistent pattern of shortages on your till will trigger an investigation.
