## 📖 Lesson 2.1 — Types of Tills Used in Zimbabwe (45 minutes)

### 1. Traditional Cash Register
- Found in small shops and tuckshops
- Has a fixed keypad with price keys
- Prints a simple receipt or uses carbon copy

### 2. Electronic Cash Register (ECR)
- More advanced than traditional
- Has a display screen
- Can track sales by department

### 3. Point of Sale (POS) System
- Full computer-based system
- Used in supermarkets like TM Pick n Pay, OK, Choppies
- Connected to stock management software
- Accepts cash, card, EcoCash, OneMoney, Zipit

### 4. Mobile POS (mPOS)
- Used on tablets or phones
- Common in small businesses and market vendors
- Examples: iKhokha, Paynow terminal

## 📖 Lesson 2.2 — Parts of a POS Terminal (60 minutes)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           POS SYSTEM COMPONENTS             │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────────┤
│ HARDWARE           │ PURPOSE                │
├────────────────────┼────────────────────────┤
│ Monitor/Screen     │ Display transactions   │
│ Customer Display   │ Shows price to buyer   │
│ Barcode Scanner    │ Reads product barcodes │
│ Keyboard/Keypad    │ Manual entry           │
│ Cash Drawer        │ Stores notes & coins   │
│ Receipt Printer    │ Prints receipts        │
│ Card Machine       │ Swipe/tap card payment │
│ CPU/Computer       │ Runs the software      │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘
```

### The Cash Drawer
- Has 5 note slots (usually: $1, $2, $5, $10, $20 or USD denominations)
- Has coin slots at the top section
- Opens automatically when a cash payment is processed
- Should NEVER be forced open manually

### The Barcode Scanner
- Reads the black and white lines (barcode) on products
- The number beneath the barcode is called the **EAN** (European Article Number)
- If scanner fails, you can type the barcode number manually

## 📖 Lesson 2.3 — Logging Into a POS System (45 minutes)

### Step-by-Step Login Process
1. Power on the POS terminal
2. Wait for the system to fully load (do not rush this)
3. The login screen will appear
4. Enter your **Cashier ID** (given by your employer)
5. Enter your **PIN/Password**
6. Press **Enter** or **Login**
7. The system will show your name and shift start time
8. Check that the till has correct float (opening cash)
9. You are now ready to serve customers

### Important Security Rules
- Never share your cashier ID or PIN with anyone
- If you suspect someone knows your PIN, report to supervisor immediately
- Always log OUT before leaving the till, even for a toilet break
- Never use another cashier's login

## 📖 Lesson 2.4 — Scanning Products & Manual Entry (45 minutes)

### Scanning with a Barcode Scanner
1. Pick up the product
2. Point the scanner at the barcode
3. You will hear a **BEEP** — this means it scanned successfully
4. The product name and price appear on screen
5. Place the item in the packing area

### When the Barcode Won't Scan
- Clean the scanner glass with a cloth
- Try scanning from a different angle
- If still failing, type the barcode number manually on the keypad
- If no barcode exists, call a supervisor to price check

### Price Override
- If an item scans at the wrong price, do NOT process it
- Call a supervisor for a **price check**
- Do not guess the price — this causes stock losses
